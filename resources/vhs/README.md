# Demo recordings

The GIFs in `resources/demo-*.gif` are generated with [VHS](https://github.com/charmbracelet/vhs) from the tapes in this folder. Replace `<masstin dir>` and `<evidence dir>` with your paths and run `vhs demo-hunt.tape`.

- `demo-parse.tape`: `parse-windows` over the [EVTX-to-MITRE-Attack](https://github.com/mdecrevoisier/EVTX-to-MITRE-Attack) samples.
- `demo-hunt.tape`: `graph-hunt-csv` with a seed over the timeline of the [DFIR Madness "Szechuan sauce"](https://dfirmadness.com/the-stolen-szechuan-sauce/) case.
